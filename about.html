Our Story

Started in 2010 with five tables and a single grill, The Kings Menu grew on word-of-mouth and a promise: every guest leaves with a favorite story to tell.

Today we pair that same hospitality with a smart digital menu, letting you see what is fresh, place orders quickly, and savor more time together.
